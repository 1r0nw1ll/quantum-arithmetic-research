## New Finds This Window

### 1. arXiv:2607.13749 — "Algebraic Representability as the Limiting Regime of Grokking: An Exactly Solvable Model with Holomorphic Activations"

**Authors**: Chon-Fai Kam, Xavier Cadet, Miloud Bessafi, Frederic Cadet (Université des Antilles, Guadeloupe)
**Submitted**: July 14, 2026
**Categories**: cs.LG, stat.ML
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13749
**Rating**: LOW-MEDIUM
**Status**: MEGA-SLIP — missed scans #21, #22, #23, #24 (4 consecutive)

**Summary**: Provides an exactly solvable theoretical model for understanding when grokking occurs in neural networks trained on modular arithmetic. Uses holomorphic activations z^d (complex polynomial degree d) instead of ReLU, giving closed-form analysis. Main result: **Algebraic Representability Criterion** — a holomorphic degree-d network can represent f: Z/mZ → ℂ if and only if f's Fourier spectrum lies in the span of {χ^k : 0 ≤ k ≤ d}, where χ is the fundamental character of Z/mZ. Non-representable tasks fail at the training accuracy level (network cannot fit the training set regardless of compute). Representable tasks are learned instantly with no grokking delay. Validated across 585 training runs (five activation degrees × thirty-nine modular tasks × three seeds): 99.8% prediction accuracy. Contrast: ReLU networks memorize every task, then exhibit textbook grokking delay of 3,700–7,500 gradient steps on tasks the holomorphic model cannot represent.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW-MEDIUM**:
- **Fourier-domain representability ↔ QA orbit character structure**: The paper's key criterion (f is representable by holomorphic degree-d network iff Fourier spectrum ⊆ span{χ^0, ..., χ^d}) is a structural statement about which functions on Z/mZ are "character-simple" in the sense that they lie in the submodule spanned by the first d+1 characters of Z/mZ. QA's orbit partition (Cosmos/Satellite/Singularity) corresponds exactly to the three distinct character types of Z/9Z or Z/24Z: trivial character (Singularity), Satellite characters (order dividing 8), and Cosmos characters (order dividing 24 but not 8). The paper's framework formalizes the "character complexity" of a modular function — adjacent to the scan #17 HIGH papers.
- **Distinction from scan #17 HIGHs**: The scan #17 cluster (arXiv:2606.02993, arXiv:2606.08985, arXiv:2606.23044) established that NNs *converge* to character orbits. This paper establishes a prior question: which functions *can be represented* by polynomial activations at all. Together they form a logically complete picture: representability (this paper) → convergence to irreps (arXiv:2606.02993) → geometry of learned representation (arXiv:2606.08985). The representability layer was missing from the prior chain.
- **Holomorphic activation = Q-algebra multiplication**: The holomorphic degree-d activation z^d corresponds to d-fold tensor product of the fundamental representation of Z/mZ. This is isomorphic to multiplying characters: χ^d is the d-th power character. QA's step operator σ acts by adding the fundamental orbit generator — a degree-1 holomorphic operation in this framework. QA's T2 Firewall (continuous functions never enter as QA causal inputs) has a representability analog: only functions whose Fourier spectrum is "character-simple" can be represented by polynomial activations acting on Z/mZ.
- **Degree-d activations ↔ orbit orders**: For Z/24Z, the orbit orders are 1 (Singularity), 8 (Satellite), 24 (Cosmos). A holomorphic degree-1 activation can only represent functions whose Fourier spectrum spans {χ^0, χ^1} — only the first two characters. To represent a function with support on the full Cosmos orbit (all 24 characters), one needs degree 23 or higher. This connects to the scan #5 HIGH (arXiv:2605.11589): DFT is the unique matched transform for Z/24Z precisely because it takes all 24 characters; polynomial approximations of insufficient degree cannot represent the full orbit structure.
- **Not a standalone cert candidate**: The representability result is an adjacent theoretical result, not a new certifiable QA claim. It adds to the [535] `qa_nn_group_spectral_cert_v1` evidence as a theoretical complement to arXiv:2606.02993's convergence result. May also add to [534] `qa_pfe_crt_character_cert_v1` evidence (CRT decomposition controls which character subspaces are representable at each prime channel).
- **Root cause of mega-slip**: Non-standard author cluster (Université des Antilles, Guadeloupe). Not in major US/EU author clusters covered by standard search. Root cause identical to IGE cluster (Ngaoundere, Cameroon) in scans #21–23.
- **Verdict**: LOW-MEDIUM. Theoretical complement to scan #17 HIGH cluster. Adds the "representability" layer to the grokking→character→orbit chain. No new cert; adds to [534]+[535] evidence.
