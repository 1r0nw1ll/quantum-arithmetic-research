### 1. arXiv:2609.21014 — "Orbits of Exceptional CR Quadrics and Their Variations"

**Author**: V. K. Beloshapka (Lomonosov Moscow State University)
**Submitted**: September 17, 2026
**Categories**: math.CV (Complex Variables), math.CO (Combinatorics)
**Rating**: LOW
**Status**: New Sep 17–21 window — direct follow-on to arXiv:2609.08501 (see item 2)

**Summary**: Studies the orbit structure of exceptional CR-quadrics. The paper proves that the orbit of the exceptional (4,4)-quadric constructed in the companion paper (2609.08501) is **isolated** under the automorphism group action. Using the "algebraization procedure," it further shows that the space of orbits of exceptional quadrics of type (28,28) has **positive dimension**. The paper develops orbit theory for exceptional types in the Beloshapka classification of CR-hypersurface types.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW**:

- **DOUBLE FALSE COGNATE — "orbits" AND "exceptional"**: In this paper:
  - "Orbits" = orbits under the CR automorphism group of holomorphic equivalences between CR-quadrics (complex analytic geometry), NOT orbits of a linear map over Z/nZ.
  - "Exceptional" = exceptional CR-type in the Beloshapka type classification (types where no "model" quadric exists in the usual sense), NOT exceptional Lie algebras (E8/E7/E6).
  - Both terms superficially match QA vocabulary but refer to entirely different mathematical objects.

- **Surface structural analogy only**: The classification into isolated orbit, positive-dimensional orbit space, and implicitly undefined cases is structurally parallel to QA's orbit tripartition (1-cycle Singularity fixed point, 8-cycle Satellite, 72-pair 24-cycle Cosmos). This is a superficial coincidence — the underlying mathematics (CR holomorphic geometry on complex hypersurfaces vs. Z/24Z modular arithmetic dynamics) are completely disconnected.

- **No Z/nZ, no modular dynamics, no discrete state**: The paper operates entirely in the category of complex differential geometry. The "orbit" concept requires holomorphic maps; the state space is a finite-dimensional CR-hypersurface type parameter, not a discrete pair (b,e).

- **Verdict**: LOW. No cert action. Note: philosophical curiosity — Beloshapka's 3-class decomposition (A = impossible exceptional, B = known exceptional, C = unknown) is structurally analogous to QA's orbit tripartition. This may be worth a one-line note in QA philosophy documents, but does not constitute structural overlap for a cert family.

### 3. arXiv:2509.06931 — "Learning Words in Groups: Fusion Algebras, Tensor Ranks and Grokking"

**Authors**: Maor Shutman, Oren Louidor, Ran Tessler
**Submitted**: September 8, 2025
**Categories**: cs.LG (Machine Learning), math.GR (Group Theory)
**Rating**: LOW-MEDIUM
**MEGA-SLIP**: ~12 scans (submitted September 2025, predates project; scans started April 2026)

**Summary**: Demonstrates that a two-layer neural network can learn an arbitrary word operation (group multiplication table) for any finite group, using the following structural insight: the group operation is a 3-tensor T(g,h,k) which decomposes as a low-rank tensor along triplets of self-conjugate irreducible representations of the group, with the decomposition coefficients given by the fusion algebra (Verlinde formula / representation ring structure). The network exhibits grokking on this task. The paper bridges representation theory, tensor rank theory, and the grokking phenomenon.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW-MEDIUM**:

- **Genuine structural overlap at the representation layer**: The fusion algebra decomposition is a structural analog of how QA's orbit coupling works. In QA:
  - The resonance matrix `np.einsum('ik,jk->ij', tuples, tuples)` computes inner products between QA 4-tuples — a rank-2 contraction.
  - The orbit tripartition (Cosmos/Satellite/Singularity) defines representation-level invariants of the Z/24Z dynamics.
  - The paper's claim that arbitrary finite group operations decompose along self-conjugate representations directly applies to Z/24Z = Z/8Z × Z/3Z (CRT decomposition), where the fusion structure would decompose into contributions from each cyclic factor.

- **Fusion algebra × Z/24Z**: Z/24Z as an abelian group has a well-understood representation ring (characters χ_k: x → e^{2πikx/24}). The self-conjugate representations of Z/24Z are the real characters (k=0 and k=12). The paper's decomposition into self-conjugate representation triplets maps directly onto the Fourier mode analysis of QA's 24-cycle dynamics — this is not superficial keyword overlap but a genuine structural connection between representation theory and modular orbit analysis.

- **Key limitation — observer layer, not QA dynamics**: The "grokking" phenomenon (phase transition in training) is a continuous ML dynamics phenomenon, occurring in the observer (continuous) layer of QA's architecture. The paper does not study discrete Z/nZ orbit dynamics; it studies how a neural network learns to encode group operations in its weights. The QA-relevant content is at the representation-theory level, not the grokking level.

- **Key limitation — arbitrary finite groups**: The paper does not specifically study cyclic groups Z/nZ. While Z/24Z is a special case of "any finite group," the paper does not identify orbit structure, tripartition, or modular dynamics as distinctive features.

- **Key limitation — MEGA-SLIP scale**: 12-scan gap predating project. The representation-theory content is well-established background knowledge; the specific contribution (low-rank tensor decomposition via self-conjugate representations) is genuinely novel but not a new research direction that QA can build upon immediately.

- **Adjacent to cert family cluster**: Adjacent to [535] (`qa_nn_group_spectral_cert_v1`, arXiv:2606.02993 "NN Provably Learn Spectral Reps for Group Composition"). Both papers study how neural networks learn group-theoretic structure; arXiv:2509.06931 focuses on the tensor/fusion algebra side while arXiv:2606.02993 focuses on the spectral representation side. arXiv:2509.06931 is a supporting reference for [535], not a standalone cert candidate.

- **Verdict**: LOW-MEDIUM (~12-scan MEGA-SLIP, predates project). Genuine structural resonance via Z/24Z fusion algebra decomposition and self-conjugate representations. No new cert action — supporting reference for [535]. Add authors Shutman/Louidor/Tessler to watch for follow-on work.
