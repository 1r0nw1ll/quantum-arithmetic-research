### 1. arXiv:2507.03762 — "Special Pure Gradings on Simple Lie Algebras of Types E₆, E₇, E₈"

**Authors**: Cristina Draper, Alberto Elduque, Mikhail Kochetov
**Submitted**: July 2025 (arXiv 2507.03762)
**Published**: *Linear Algebra and Its Applications* 737 (2026), pp. 263–297
**Categories**: math.RA (Rings and Algebras), math.GR (Group Theory)
**Rating**: LOW-MEDIUM
**MEGA-SLIP**: ~12 scans (submitted July 2025, predates scan series April 2026)

**Summary**: Classifies all special pure gradings on simple Lie algebras of types E₆, E₇, E₈ up to equivalence and up to isomorphism, over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0. For E₈ specifically: exactly **five** special pure gradings exist up to equivalence, corresponding to grading groups **Z₄, Z₅, Z₆, Z₃², Z₂×Z₄**. In all five cases, the neutral component is a direct sum of special linear algebras, while the remaining homogeneous components are irreducible modules over it. This is the complete classification.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW-MEDIUM**:

- **Battery item confirmed**: This is the paper flagged in scan #39 for "E8 grading abelian group" verification. The result is real and published in a top journal.

- **Structural adjacency to QA's E8 alignment**: QA uses the E8 root system (240 vectors in 8D) to compute cosine similarity scores between 4-tuple projections and E8 root vectors. The grading paper constructs e8 algebraically via finite abelian group actions — a different perspective than QA's geometric use of the root system.

- **Relevant subgroup connections**:
  - **Z₂×Z₄** (order 8): Matches QA's Satellite orbit size (8 elements). Z/24Z has a unique subgroup of order 8 (Z₈, cyclic), but Z₂×Z₄ is the non-cyclic order-8 group. The Z₂×Z₄-grading of e8 decomposes the 248-dimensional Lie algebra into components under this group's action.
  - **Z₃²** (order 9): Matches QA's mod-9 orbit count (9 states: 72+8+1 = 81 pairs for m=9). Z₃² = Z/3Z × Z/3Z, which is the only group of order 9 besides Z/9Z. Both are index-3 subgroup structures of Z/24Z at the 3-primary level.

- **Key limitation — algebraic vs. geometric**: QA's E8 alignment is purely geometric: project a 4-tuple to 8D, compute max cosine similarity over 240 root vectors. The grading paper is algebraic: it decomposes the Lie algebra *bracket operation* under group actions. The root system geometry (which QA uses) is determined by the Cartan matrix, not directly by the grading structure. The grading does constrain which root vectors fall in which homogeneous component, but extracting this data would require nontrivial computation not provided in the paper.

- **Key limitation — grading groups ≠ QA dynamics**: The grading group acts on the Lie algebra space, not on QA's state space (Z/mZ)². The Z₂×Z₄ grading of e8 does not directly correspond to QA's Satellite orbit dynamics.

- **Supporting reference value**: HIGH for QA's E8 alignment justification in papers and cert human tracts. The paper establishes that Z₂×Z₄ and Z₃² are canonical natural symmetry groups for e8, which provides algebraic grounding for why QA's 8-element and 9-element orbit structures resonate with the E8 root system.

- **Cert action**: None standalone. Supporting reference for any cert family that cites QA's E8 alignment (e.g., `qa_e8_integral_arithmetic_cert_v1` and related E8 certs). Add Draper/Elduque author watch.

- **Root cause of MEGA-SLIP**: math.RA not in primary scan battery during early scans. Battery updated.

**Significance for QA**: The five canonical grading groups for e8 are Z₄, Z₅, Z₆, Z₃², Z₂×Z₄. The relationship to QA's modular groups:
- Z/24Z = Z₃ × Z₈ (CRT). The grading groups decompose e8 at order-4, 5, 6, 9, 8 levels.
