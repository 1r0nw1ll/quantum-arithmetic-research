### 2. arXiv:2609.00733 — "Finite permutation groups that act with fixity 4"

**Authors**: Barbara Baumeister, Paula Hähndel, Kay Magaard, Christoph Möller, Rebecca Waldecker
**Submitted**: September 1, 2026
**Categories**: math.GR (Group Theory)
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00733
**Rating**: LOW
**Status**: New in window

**Summary**: Motivated by the theory of Riemann surfaces and specifically the significance of Weierstrass points, the authors prove general structure results about finite groups that have a faithful transitive action with fixity 4 (fixity = max|Gᵥ| − 1, where Gᵥ is a point stabilizer). Fixity 4 means point stabilizers have order at most 5. The paper classifies many possibilities for such actions and provides examples.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW**:

- **Initial false signal**: The search snippet for this paper contained "congruences modulo 9," creating an appearance of Z/9Z relevance. On investigation, this appears to be proof-internal divisibility conditions in the group structure theorem (e.g., |G| divisible by certain primes), not a study of Z/9Z as a modular arithmetic system or cyclic orbit structure.

- **Fixity 4 vs. QA orbits**: QA's orbit tripartition (Cosmos 72 pairs, Satellite 8 pairs, Singularity 1 pair on Z/24Z) is defined by fixed-point structure of the σ-map — the QA step function. A group "acting with fixity 4" is a distinct concept (the largest point stabilizer has size ≤ 5). The fixity concept is not structurally equivalent to QA's orbit classification.

- **Riemann surface / Weierstrass point context**: The motivation is entirely from complex algebraic geometry (Weierstrass points on Riemann surfaces, which require group actions with bounded ramification). No connection to QA's modular arithmetic or digital signal processing domains.

- **Verdict**: LOW. False cognate: mod-9 in search snippet is proof-internal, not QA-adjacent. Permutation group structure theorem for fixity-4 actions has no structural overlap with QA orbit dynamics. No cert action.

### 3. arXiv:2508.18578 — "Arithmetic dynamics of a discrete Painlevé equation"

**Authors**: Nalini Joshi, Pieter Roffelsen
**Submitted**: August 26, 2025 (boundary item; appears in IOP Journal of Physics A)
**Categories**: math-ph (Mathematical Physics), nlin.SI (Exactly Solvable and Integrable Systems)
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18578
**Rating**: LOW
**Status**: Boundary item (Aug 2025 paper; not previously recorded in any scan)

**Summary**: Studies the orbits of a specific discrete Painlevé equation over finite fields F_p. Proves that orbits lie on algebraic curves whose defining polynomials are given explicitly; these curves have genus ≤ 1. This contrasts with the transcendental behavior of Painlevé equations over ℂ. The Hasse bound is verified for the number of points in each orbit.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW**:

- **Discrete dynamics over finite fields — genuine adjacency**: The paper studies orbits of a discrete map over F_p — the same structural setting as QA's σ-map on (Z/24Z)². This is the closest structural overlap: both study discrete dynamical systems on finite arithmetic domains, and both care about orbit structure (closed cycles, fixed points).

- **But: Painlevé dynamics ≠ QA dynamics**: Discrete Painlevé equations arise as rational surface maps (birational automorphisms of rational surfaces). They are specific integrable deformations of Möbius transformations. QA's σ-map is a linear affine map (b, e) → (e, d mod m). The Painlevé map is nonlinear and birational; QA's map is linear and everywhere-defined. The orbit structure in the Painlevé case (genus-≤-1 algebraic curves) is incompatible with QA's explicit orbit counting (72 + 8 + 1 = 81 pairs for mod 9).

- **Finite fields F_p vs. Z/24Z**: The paper uses finite fields (F_p, char p prime). QA's primary moduli are Z/9Z = Z/3²Z and Z/24Z = Z/2³·3Z — composite moduli, specifically prime-power rings, not prime-order fields. The structure theory differs (rings vs. fields).

- **No orbit tripartition**: Painlevé orbits form algebraic curves of genus ≤ 1 — not a discrete tripartition into orbit classes. The three-orbit structure (Cosmos/Satellite/Singularity) is special to QA's linear σ-map.

- **Verdict**: LOW. Discrete dynamics over finite arithmetic structures is genuine adjacency, but Painlevé dynamics (integrable birational rational surface maps) are structurally distinct from QA's linear Z/24Z shift in ways that prevent meaningful cert scaffolding. No cert action. Could add as a background reference in any future cert covering discrete dynamics over finite rings — but not standalone.
