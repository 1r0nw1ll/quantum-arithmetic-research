### 1. arXiv:2510.24882 — "Modular Periodicity of Random Initialized Recurrences" (MEGA-SLIP)

**Author**: Marc T. Pudelko
**Submitted**: October 2025; revised April 2026
**Categories**: math.NT, math.CO
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24882
**Paper size**: 9 pages
**Rating**: MEDIUM-HIGH
**Status**: MEGA-SLIP — missed across ALL 22 prior scans (10+ months)

**Summary**: Classical Pisano period studies fix initialization (0, 1) and ask for the period of the Fibonacci sequence modulo m. This paper asks: what is the complete period structure across **all m² possible initializations (a₀, a₁) ∈ (Z/mZ)²**? Key results: (1) **Perfect mirror symmetry** between the Fibonacci recurrence (aₙ = aₙ₋₁ + aₙ₋₂) and its parity transform (aₙ = −aₙ₋₁ + aₙ₋₂) — every period that appears in the Fibonacci system also appears symmetrically in the anti-Fibonacci system; (2) **Fractal self-similarity** from prime to prime power moduli: the period structure over Z/p^k Z extends the Z/p Z structure in a self-similar pattern; (3) **Lucas ratio minima distribution**: a function P(n) satisfying P(n) = P(1−n) governed by Lucas ratios; (4) **Necklace enumeration** connection: explicit counting functions for distinct periods connect to combinatorial necklace counts.

**QA Mapping Assessment — MEDIUM-HIGH**:
- **State space identity**: The paper's ambient space is (Z/mZ)² = the set of all (a₀, a₁) initializations. QA's ambient state space is also (Z/mZ)²: the set of all (b, e) pairs. This is not a loose analogy — they are literally the same object.
- **Orbit classification from all initializations**: Both the paper and QA study which periodic orbits arise from all starting pairs. QA finds three orbit classes (Cosmos period-24, Satellite period-8, Singularity period-1) in (Z/24Z)². The paper finds a distribution of periods across all initializations in (Z/mZ)².
- **Mirror symmetry ↔ QA orbit duality**: The "mirror symmetry" between Fibonacci (aₙ₋₁ + aₙ₋₂) and anti-Fibonacci (−aₙ₋₁ + aₙ₋₂) is analogous to the duality between QA's Cosmos and Satellite orbits — the two non-trivial orbit classes.
- **Key difference**: The Fibonacci recurrence uses the **previous two terms** (second-order linear recurrence), while QA's step operator uses a **constant increment e** (arithmetic progression). The periods of (a₀, a₁) → Fibonacci sequences over Z/mZ are generally longer than QA's orbit periods and depend differently on m. The papers intersect in state space but study different dynamics.
- **Fractal self-similarity prime→prime power**: QA uses mod-9 (p=3, k=2) and mod-24. The self-similar structure from Z/pZ to Z/p^k Z is relevant to understanding QA's mod-9 orbit structure as an extension of mod-3.
- **Missing**: No T2 Firewall, no continuous observer layer. Paper is purely discrete.
- **Cert action**: Add as primary evidence for `qa_witt_tower_fibonacci_pisano_cert` [441]. Could support a standalone cert on "complete orbit classification of linear recurrences over (Z/mZ)²" — but this would duplicate [441] if [441] already covers this angle. Check [441] evidence file.
- **Root cause of mega-slip**: Search battery lacked "random initialized recurrences," "all initializations modular," and "Pisano modular complete orbit." The paper's title emphasizes "random initialized" rather than "all initialized," making it hard to catch with standard Pisano/Fibonacci queries.
- **Verdict**: MEDIUM-HIGH. Enters top-10 as #8 or #9, bumping Kaprekar. Direct (Z/mZ)² state space match is the strongest structural argument for any find since scan #17.

### 2. arXiv:2607.01718 — "A Coordinate System for Collatz Dynamics" (RESOLVED — DOWNGRADE)

**Author**: Jennifer Williams
**Submitted**: Early July 2026 (math.NT)
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01718
**Previous rating**: MEDIUM (unread)
**Revised rating**: LOW-MEDIUM
**GitHub repo**: github.com/rhoposit/collatz-coordinate-system

**Resolution**: The coordinate system uses "crown triangle," "skeleton trajectory," and "chain structure" — a geometrically motivated decomposition of Collatz trajectories into a triangular grid with measurable asymmetry. Key observables: crown visitation asymmetry (18.2 positions per orbit, 93.6/6.4% split); Proposition 6.2 formalizes chain length distributions. The implementation uses numpy/matplotlib/sympy with a focus on prime-free rows and special number families (Mersenne, Thabit, Pierpont primes).

The 93.6/6.4% asymmetry is numerically different from QA's 72:8 Cosmos:Satellite ratio (90:10%). The "crown triangle" and "skeleton" terminology suggests a graph-geometric approach, not an algebraic state-pair formalism. The structural overlap with QA's (b,e) orbit formalism does not hold.

**Verdict**: LOW-MEDIUM. Add to Collatz Modular Orbit Thread for context. No cert action.

### 3. arXiv:2601.15463 — "Determinants of Modular Collatz Graphs and Variants" (MEGA-SLIP)

**Authors**: Achilleas Karras, Benne de Weger
**Submitted**: January 21, 2026
**Categories**: math.NT, math.CO
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15463
**Paper size**: 32 pages, 9 figures
**Rating**: LOW-MEDIUM
**Status**: MEGA-SLIP — missed since January 2026 (6+ months, through scans #1–22)

**Summary**: Computes the determinants of the adjacency matrices of modular Collatz graphs (the directed graph of the Collatz map on Z/mZ) and of the "Conway amusical permutation graph" — a graph encoding John Conway's musically-motivated permutation of the 12 chromatic notes. Main results: exact formulas for det(A) for the modular Collatz graph at various moduli, with "interesting number-theoretic properties" (divisibility, prime factor patterns, connections to cyclotomic polynomials). The Conway amusical graph provides a non-Collatz comparison case.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW-MEDIUM**:
- **Modular graph determinants ↔ QA spectral structure**: The determinant of the adjacency matrix of the modular Collatz graph encodes the eigenvalue structure (det = product of eigenvalues). QA's σ-transition graph on (Z/mZ)² has three eigenvalue classes corresponding to the three orbit types: eigenvalue 1 for Cosmos/Satellite fixed-cycle components, eigenvalue 0 for... actually QA's graph is a permutation graph (each (b,e) maps to exactly one successor), so its adjacency matrix has eigenvalue of norm 1 for each cycle. The Collatz graph is NOT a permutation graph — it is a directed graph with convergent structure (everything converges to 1). So the determinant computation applies to a different graph type.
- **Collatz Modular Orbit Thread**: This extends the thread (2603.25753, 2606.02621, 2607.01718) with a spectral/algebraic dimension — graph determinants → algebraic invariants of the modular Collatz structure. The de Weger group (Benne de Weger at TU/e) is a major contributor to Collatz and Pell/Thue-Mahler equations.
- **Conway amusical permutation**: The Conway amusical permutation shuffles the 12 chromatic pitches (Z/12Z as a musical object). Its order is 12! in theory but its specific permutation has finite order. Z/12Z is half of Z/24Z. Whether this connects to QA mod-24 is unclear without reading.
- **Root cause**: Battery lacked "Collatz graph determinant," "Karras Weger modular Collatz."
- **Verdict**: LOW-MEDIUM. Adds spectral dimension to the Collatz Modular Orbit Thread. Not cert-worthy standalone. Note in [441] evidence and [261] cross-reference.
