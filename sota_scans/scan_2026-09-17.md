### 1. arXiv:2609.16178 — "The Mathieu Property for Compact Connected Lie Groups"

**Author**: Christopher D. Long
**Submitted**: September 2026
**Categories**: math.RT / math.GR (Representation Theory, Group Theory)
**Rating**: LOW
**Status**: New Sep 14–17 window

**Summary**: Proves that the kernel of normalized Haar integration on the algebra of representative functions of a compact connected Lie group G is a Mathieu–Zhao subspace if and only if G is a torus. The paper includes a Lean 4 formalization (GitHub: long-mathematics/mathieu-property-compact-connected-lie-groups), with kernel-checked proofs of the Hopf coefficient theorem, sphere marker tower, and universal radial-transfer theorem, plus an explicit SU(2) counterexample.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW**:

- **FALSE NAME COGNATE — "Mathieu Property" ≠ Mathieu groups**: The Mathieu property (Mathieu–Zhao subspace theory) is a problem in invariant theory about whether kernels of certain linear functionals are Mathieu–Zhao subspaces. It is ENTIRELY UNRELATED to the Mathieu sporadic simple groups M₁₁, M₁₂, M₂₂, M₂₃, M₂₄ that are relevant to IGP24/[506].

- **No orbit dynamics**: The paper studies whether a subspace of a function algebra satisfies a structural property under Haar integration. No cyclic group, no Z/nZ, no σ-map iteration, no orbit tripartition.

- **Lean formalization notable but not QA-cert-relevant**: The Lean 4 formalization is interesting for the QA formalization effort broadly, but the mathematical content has no QA structural overlap.

- **Verdict**: LOW. No cert action.

### 6. arXiv:2506.20118 — "The Graph Structure of a Class of Permutation Maps over Ring Z_{p^k}"

**Authors**: Kai Tan, Chengqing Li
**Submitted**: June 25, 2025
**Categories**: math.NT / cs.IT (Number Theory, Information Theory)
**Rating**: LOW-MEDIUM
**MEGA-SLIP**: ~38 scans (predates project; submitted June 2025, project scans started April 2026)
**Root cause**: Pre-project paper; scan battery covers 2026 submissions. Not in geographic or author watch.

**Summary**: Studies the functional graph structure of iterative applications of a class of permutation maps f on Z_{p^k} (residue rings modulo p^k). For each map f, the paper describes the cycle structure (length and number of cycles) and rho structure (tail length before entering a cycle) of the functional graph x → f(x) → f(f(x)) → ... over Z/p^k. Results include explicit formulas for cycle lengths in terms of p, k, and the defining parameters of f. Cross-listed to cs.IT (coding theory), suggesting applications to pseudorandom sequence design.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW-MEDIUM**:

- **Genuine structural overlap with QA orbit analysis**: This paper directly studies the functional graph of iterative maps over Z/p^k — exactly the type of object QA analyzes when classifying orbits of the map σ: (b,e) → ((b+e-1)%m+1, e) on Z/mZ pairs. The cycle structure (period, rho tail, fixed points) is directly analogous to QA's orbit tripartition (24-cycle Cosmos, 8-cycle Satellite, 1-cycle Singularity).

- **Z/9Z = Z/3² overlap**: Z/9Z (QA's theoretical modulus) is exactly Z/p^k with p=3, k=2. The paper's results apply directly to Z/9Z. Adjacent to existing HIGH cert cluster [537] (`qa_linear_fd_galois_ring_cert_v1`, arXiv:2604.01548 "Linear FDS over Galois Rings (Z/9Z)").

- **Key limitation — prime power only**: Z/24Z ≅ Z/8Z × Z/3Z (by CRT) is NOT a prime power. The paper does not handle composite moduli directly. QA's primary modulus (Z/24Z) is outside scope; the secondary modulus (Z/9Z) is in scope.

- **Key limitation — univariate maps**: QA studies pairs (b,e) as state. This paper studies univariate maps x → f(x) over Z/p^k, not bivariate (b,e) dynamics. The structural analogy holds, but the paper does not directly address QA's 2D state space.

- **Pre-project MEGA-SLIP**: Submitted June 2025, predating the QA scan project entirely. The scan battery covers 2026 submissions; this paper falls outside the design window.

- **Verdict**: LOW-MEDIUM (37+ scan mega-slip, predates project). Genuine structural resonance at Z/9Z level; adjacent to [537]. No new cert action — supporting reference for [537] at most. Add to author watch for follow-on work.

### 7. Scientific Reports (April 2026) — "Applications of Lucas Sequences in Convergence and Signal Processing"

**Journal**: Scientific Reports (Nature)
**Published**: April 2026
**DOI**: 10.1038/s41598-026-43030-9
**Rating**: LOW-MEDIUM
**MEGA-SLIP**: ~17 scans (published April 2026; first sighted scan #38)
**Root cause**: Journal publication, not arXiv; scan battery misses non-arXiv signal processing publications.

**Summary**: Defines λ-Lucas statistical convergence and strongly λ-Lucas summability through modulus functions. The "Lucas transform" is an embedding of the Lucas number sequence (generalizing Fibonacci) into statistical convergence theory. The paper defines new difference sequence spaces based on Lucas numbers modulo various parameters, derives inclusions/equivalences with classical statistical convergence theory, and demonstrates applications in adaptive filters and spectral signal filtering with numerical simulations on noisy sinusoidal signals and blurred images.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW-MEDIUM**:

- **Structural bridge — Lucas sequences and modular recurrences**: Lucas sequences V_n(P,Q) satisfy V_{n} = P·V_{n-1} - Q·V_{n-2}, directly generalizing the Fibonacci recurrence. The periodicity of Lucas sequences mod m is governed by the Lucas-Pisano period λ(m) (analogous to the Fibonacci Pisano period π(m)). QA's Z/24Z dynamics are governed by related periodicity structure.

- **Signal processing application domain**: The paper's application domain (adaptive filtering, spectral signal processing) is directly within QA's applied domain. QA already has cert families in signal processing (qa_witt_tower family, filter bank experiments). This paper bridges the discrete recurrence / modular periodicity side with the signal processing application side.

- **Key limitation — summability theory, not orbit dynamics**: The paper's "convergence" is sequence convergence in the functional analysis / summability theory sense (does a sequence satisfy a generalized convergence criterion via Lucas weighting?), NOT convergence of orbits in a dynamical system. The paper does not study orbits of linear maps on Z/nZ. The modular structure is in the background (Lucas sequences mod m define the basis), not the foreground.

- **Key limitation — continuous signal processing**: The adaptive filter and spectral filtering results operate on continuous/real-valued signal processing, consistent with QA's observer-layer architecture. But the paper does not use Lucas-mod-m as a discrete state layer; it uses Lucas numbers as a continuous summability kernel.

- **Verdict**: LOW-MEDIUM (~17-scan mega-slip from April 2026). Domain bridge paper (Lucas periodicity + signal processing). No cert action. Add to QA signal processing bibliography. Battery update: add Scientific Reports / Nature journals to scan sources for Lucas/Fibonacci/modular recurrence cross-domain papers.
