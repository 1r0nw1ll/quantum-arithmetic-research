### 1. arXiv:2609.05932 — "Finite-Sample Rigidity for Left-Translated Similarity Orbits and Off-Diagonal Quadratic Realizations"

**Author**: Hiroki Minamide
**Submitted**: September 5, 2026
**Categories**: math.RA (Rings and Algebras)
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05932
**Rating**: LOW
**Status**: New in Sep 7–10 window

**Summary**: Studies finite-sample rigidity for left translates of similarity orbits over commutative rings. Given matrices G_i = FK_i where F is a common invertible matrix and the K_i lie in the similarity orbit of a cyclic matrix C (a companion matrix), determines when a finite translated sample has exactly the same multiplier ambiguity as the complete orbit. Main structural result: orbit differences span the special linear Lie algebra, yielding a scalar left-stabilizer description and a determinantal saturation criterion. Off-diagonal quadratic operators on pairs of free rank-2 modules realize the translated-orbit model exactly.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW**:

- **"Cyclic matrix" ≠ Z/nZ cyclic group**: The paper uses "cyclic matrix" to mean a companion matrix (one with a cyclic vector — a single vector that spans the full space under repeated action of A). This is standard linear algebra terminology, not a reference to the cyclic group Z/nZ or to QA's modular dynamics.

- **Similarity orbits ≠ QA orbit tripartition**: The "orbit" here is the similarity orbit of a matrix under conjugation (GL_n-action on matrices), not the orbit of a (b,e) pair under QA's σ-map on Z/24Z. These are structurally distinct mathematical objects.

- **Commutative ring scope doesn't include Z/24Z context**: While Z/24Z is a commutative ring and technically in scope, the paper studies rank-2 free modules and GL_2-similarity, which doesn't produce the orbit tripartition structure QA needs.

- **Verdict**: LOW. Double false cognate on "cyclic" and "orbit." No cert action.

### 3. arXiv:2609.04993 — "The Right Edge of the Zero Set of the Fibonacci Zeta Function"

**Author**: Marco Mantovanelli
**Submitted**: September 4, 2026
**Categories**: math.NT (Number Theory)
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04993
**Rating**: LOW
**Status**: New in Sep 7–10 window; included for Fibonacci keyword audit

**Summary**: Defines the Fibonacci zeta function Z_F(s) = Σ F_n^{-s} for Re s > 0 (a Dirichlet series over Fibonacci numbers). Determines the exact right edge σ_F ≈ 0.74316... of the closure of real parts of zeros, given by the equation Z_F(σ_F) = 4 + 2·144^{-σ_F} (note: 144 = F_{12}). Proves Z_F(s) ≠ 0 for Re s ≥ σ_F; the zero set accumulates almost-periodically to the left of σ_F with growing-dimensional phase locking.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW**:

- **Analytic NT, not modular orbit dynamics**: The Fibonacci zeta function is an analytic number theory object — a Dirichlet series whose analytic continuation and zero structure are studied in the complex plane. This is entirely distinct from QA's use of Fibonacci recurrences, which appears in the Pisano period context (Fibonacci mod m orbit classification).

- **"Fibonacci" false cognate**: QA uses F_n to classify orbit periods mod m (Pisano period π(m)), where the sequence {F_n mod m} is periodic. This paper studies the complex function Σ F_n^{-s}, a Dirichlet series whose zeros encode analytic structure. No modular arithmetic involved.

- **No orbit tripartition, no Z/24Z**: The result σ_F ≈ 0.74316... encodes nothing about the Pisano period π(24) = 24 or π(9) = 24.

- **Verdict**: LOW. False cognate on "Fibonacci." No cert action.

### 4. arXiv:2606.31900 — "Galois Extensions via Finiteness of Orbits"

**Author**: Nikolaos Marmaridis
**Submitted**: June 30, 2026
**Categories**: math.GR (Group Theory)

**Rating**: LOW
**MEGA-SLIP**: ~19 scans (submitted June 30, should have been in scan #17 window June 25–July 2)

**Summary**: Presents an orbit-theoretic reformulation of Galois theory. For a field E with subgroup H ≤ Aut(E) and fixed field E^H: (1) α ∈ E is algebraic over E^H iff its H-orbit is finite; (2) the minimal polynomial of α over E^H can be constructed as the product of linear factors (x - σ(α)) over σ ∈ H-orbit, guaranteeing separability; (3) E/E^H is Galois iff all H-orbits have finite length; (4) E/E^H is a finite Galois extension iff H-orbit lengths are bounded above.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW**:

- **"Orbit" = field automorphism orbit, not Z/nZ cyclic orbit**: The orbits in this paper are H-orbits of field elements α ∈ E under automorphisms σ ∈ H ≤ Aut(E). - **Galois theory foundation, not discrete dynamics**: The paper provides an orbit-theoretic proof system for infinite Galois extensions. QA's orbit tripartition is a theorem about a specific linear map on a finite ring, not an infinite automorphism group action.

- **No modular arithmetic**: No Z/nZ content, no modular arithmetic, no cyclic group Z/mZ in the sense of number-theoretic modular rings.

- **Verdict**: LOW (19-scan mega-slip). False cognate on "orbit." No cert action. Root cause added to battery: full math.GR sweep for June–July window.

### 5. arXiv:2607.12761 — "Cyclic Projective Orbits on Rational Normal Curves and MDS Codes"

**Authors**: Yangcheng Li, Pingzhi Yuan
**Submitted**: July 14, 2026 (v2: July 15)
**Categories**: math.CO (Combinatorics), cs.IT (Information Theory), math.NT (Number Theory)
**URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12761
**Rating**: LOW-MEDIUM
**MEGA-SLIP**: ~15 scans (submitted July 14, should have been in scan #20 window July 13–16)
**Root cause**: cs.IT / math.CO cross-listing not in scan battery; author affiliation cluster (Guangzhou) absent from geographic battery.

**Summary**: Let A be a cyclic operator (companion matrix) on an r-dimensional vector space over a field k, and z a cyclic vector. The Krylov code has parity-check matrix (z, Az, ..., A^{n-1}z). For r ≥ 3 and n ≥ r+3, proves that an MDS orbit segment lies on a rational normal curve iff the projective pair (A, [z]) is conjugate to one arising from the (r-1)-st symmetric-power action of PGL₂. Over finite fields F_q, for companion operators, gives a complete classification of the generalized Reed-Solomon locus into three families: split semisimple, two nonsplit semisimple, and unipotent.

**QA Mapping Assessment — LOW-MEDIUM**:

- **Three-family classification — superficial resonance**: The split/nonsplit-semisimple/unipotent trichotomy for MDS codes has a superficial numerical correspondence with QA's orbit tripartition (Cosmos/Satellite/Singularity). However, three is a small number, and the mathematical content is entirely distinct: the Krylov code families classify linear operators over finite fields by their eigenvalue structure (split semisimple = all eigenvalues in F_q; nonsplit = eigenvalues in F_{q²}; unipotent = nilpotent part), not by orbit period or Z/nZ modular dynamics.

- **"Cyclic operator" ≠ Z/nZ cyclic group**: The "cyclic operator" here means a linear map with a cyclic vector (companion matrix), not the cyclic group Z/nZ acting on a modular ring. Different mathematical concept.

- **Over finite fields, not Z/24Z**: The paper works over finite fields F_q (prime powers). Z/24Z is not a finite field.

- **Genuine partial adjacency**: The Krylov code perspective (structured sequence of images Az, A²z, ...) is conceptually adjacent to QA's σ-orbit sequence of (b,e) pairs. Both produce structured sequences from repeated application of a single linear operator. But QA's operator is on Z/mZ (composite ring), not a vector space over F_q.

- **Verdict**: LOW-MEDIUM (15-scan mega-slip). Three-family orbit classification parallel is a genuine structural curiosity but not certifiable. Add to search battery: cs.IT / math.CO Krylov code orbit classification. No cert action.

### Matrix Orbit Taxonomy — Separate Cluster from QA

Two September 2026 papers form a "matrix orbit" signal-processing cluster:
- arXiv:2609.05932 (Minamide) — similarity orbits of cyclic/companion matrices, commutative rings

The terminology overlap ("orbit," "cyclic") is a false cognate in both cases.
